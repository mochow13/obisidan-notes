> Build the technical judgment expected of a **Staff+ backend / AI engineer**: architecture trade-offs, distributed-systems reasoning, scalability, reliability, performance, platform design, and production AI.
>
> This is a curated, deduplicated high-signal list from InfoQ presentations published in **2024, 2025, and 2026**. Talks that fit multiple categories are placed under the topic where they are most useful.

## How to use this note

For each talk, capture:
- **Problem / constraints**
- **Architecture and key design decisions**
- **Trade-offs and rejected alternatives**
- **Failure modes / operational lessons**
- **Scale numbers / SLOs / performance characteristics**
- **What I would apply differently in my own systems**
- **Staff+ lesson:** how the speaker frames ambiguity, risk, cross-team impact, or long-term evolution
